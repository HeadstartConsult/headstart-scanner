2. What personal information we process (POPIA)

Documents you scan often contain personal information, such as ID numbers, addresses or bank details. Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), Headstart Consult CC does not collect, receive, store or share that information when you use the scanner, because the processing happens only on your device and under your control.

  • No account, name, e-mail or phone number is asked for.
  • The scanner sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Your pages are held in your browser's memory only while the page is open. Closing the page or tapping "Delete all" removes them.
  • To let the scanner work offline, your browser stores a copy of the app's own program files. It never stores your scans there.
  • Like any website, our web host receives the technical details your browser sends when it loads the page, such as your IP address and browser type. These are kept in the host's standard server logs for security and are not used to identify or profile you.

If you share a PDF through another app (for example e-mail or WhatsApp), that app's own privacy terms apply.

You have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to processing. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, e-mail POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

3. Using your scans (ECT Act and other laws)

  • A scan is an electronic copy. In terms of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 an electronic copy can meet some legal requirements, but many institutions still require an original or a certified copy. A scan from this tool is not certified and does not replace one.
  • Scan only documents you are entitled to copy. Copying another person's documents or copyright works without permission may be unlawful, and altering a document to deceive anyone is an offence.
  • Check every PDF before you send it. Automatic cropping and enhancement can occasionally cut off or blur part of a page.
  • If you scan someone else's personal information, you are responsible for handling it lawfully under POPIA.
