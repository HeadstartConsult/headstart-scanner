How it works

From paper to PDF in four steps

Open the scanner in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

1

Add a page

Take a photo with your camera, or choose photos you already have. On a computer you can also drag images onto the page.

2

Check the edges

The scanner finds the page edges for you. Drag a corner to adjust, rotate the page, or use the whole photo.

3

Enhance

Pick Auto, Original, Greyscale, or Black & white to make the text easy to read.

4

Save as PDF

Add more pages, name the file, choose A4, US Letter or the scan's own size, and pick a file size. Then save it, or share it where your device allows.

Your privacy

Your documents never leave your device

IDs, payslips, leases and bank statements hold personal information. Headstart Scanner does all of its work inside your own browser, so those pages are never sent to Headstart Consult CC or to anyone else.

  • Processed on your device. Photos, crops and PDFs are made in your browser, not on a server.
  • Nothing stored by us. Headstart Consult CC does not receive, see, or keep your scans.
  • No account, no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Built with POPIA in mind. Because your documents are never collected, the personal information in them stays under your control, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013.

Good to know: like any website, our web host may keep standard technical logs (such as your IP address and browser type) when this page loads. These never include your documents. Once you save or share a PDF, keeping it safe is up to you, so delete scans from shared devices when you are done.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee, no trial period and no sign-up. The scanner runs on your own device, so there is nothing for us to charge for.

What do I need?

A phone, tablet or computer with an up-to-date web browser. To take photos directly, your device needs a camera; otherwise you can choose images you already have.

Can I scan more than one page?

Yes. Add as many pages as you need, put them in order, and save them together as one PDF.

Is a scan the same as a certified copy?

No. A scan is a copy, not an original. Where an organisation asks for a certified copy or the original document, a scan will not replace it. Only scan documents you are entitled to copy.
